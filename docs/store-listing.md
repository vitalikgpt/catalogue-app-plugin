## Long

Catalogue App (@catalogue_app) brings Telegram into your assistant.

- **Bots in one click.** Ask for a support bot, a feedback bot, a shop or paid access to
  a channel — the assistant returns a link, you confirm in Telegram, and your bot is live.
  No @BotFather, no token, no server: the bot is hosted for free by @catalogue_app and you
  can edit it later in its node editor.
- **Emoji and sticker packs.** Turn your photos, videos or GIFs into a custom emoji or
  sticker pack in one tap, or find a ready pack on any theme with an add link.
- **Search Telegram.** Mini apps, bots and TON services; more than 1.5 million public
  channels, group chats and bots with subscriber counts. Refreshed daily.
- **Widgets in chats.** Catalogue Widgets: an interactive profile card and a runner game
  sent right into any Telegram chat from @catalogue_app.
- **Bot API knowledge base.** Current notes for developers on managed bots, Mini Apps,
  packs, Telegram Stars payments, webhooks and limits.

Read-only, no sign-in. Everything that creates something finishes in Telegram, where you
confirm it.
