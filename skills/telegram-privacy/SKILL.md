# Privacy in Telegram

All of these live in **Settings → Privacy and Security**.

| Want | Setting |
|---|---|
| Hide phone number | Phone Number → "Who can see my phone number": Nobody. Below it, "Who can find me by my number": My Contacts |
| Hide online / last seen | Last Seen & Online → Nobody (or My Contacts). Others then see "last seen recently". You will not see their exact time either, unless you have Premium |
| Hide profile photo | Profile Photos → My Contacts or Nobody; exceptions can be added |
| Stop being added to groups | Groups & Channels → My Contacts |
| Hide your name on forwards | Forwarded Messages → Nobody |
| Calls | Calls → Nobody or My Contacts; peer-to-peer can be turned off to hide your IP |
| Read receipts in private chats | Last Seen & Online → "Hide read time" (with Premium you keep seeing others') |

Hide a chat without deleting it: swipe it left (or long-press) → Archive.
Archived chats with notifications muted stay archived when new messages come.

A username can be shared instead of the number: Settings → Username.
