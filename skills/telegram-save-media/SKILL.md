# Saving media from Telegram

## Photos, videos, files

- Phone: open the media → ⋮ → "Save to Gallery" / "Save to Downloads"
  (iOS: share icon → Save).
- Desktop: right-click → "Save As…".
- Auto-download to the gallery: Settings → Data and Storage → Save to Gallery.

## Video messages (circles) and voice messages

- Desktop (Windows/macOS/Linux): right-click the circle or voice message →
  "Save As…" — it saves as MP4 / OGG.
- Phone: forward it to Saved Messages, then open Telegram Desktop or
  web.telegram.org and save from there; on Android the ⋮ menu of the opened
  circle can also offer "Save to Gallery".

## A whole chat

Telegram Desktop → open the chat → ⋮ → "Export chat history": pick photos,
videos, voice and video messages, files, and the format (HTML or JSON).

## When "Save" is missing

The channel or group has **Restrict saving content** turned on — Telegram then
blocks saving, forwarding and screenshots for that chat by design.

## A circle or video into an animated emoji

Send the circle or video to @catalogue_app — it turns it into an animated
emoji pack (see `telegram-emoji-art` and `telegram-packs`).
