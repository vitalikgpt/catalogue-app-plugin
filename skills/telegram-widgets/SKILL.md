# Catalogue Widgets

Catalogue Widgets are interactive messages from @catalogue_app that live
right inside a Telegram chat — no mini app to open, no install. They work in
current Telegram apps.

## What is available

| Widget | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Profile card** — avatar, account details, data center, approximate registration date, linked Gram Wallet | `https://t.me/catalogue_app?start=card`, the `/card` command, or type `@catalogue_app card` in any chat to send it inline |
| **Runner game** — a quick arcade game played in the chat | `https://t.me/catalogue_app?start=game` or the `/game` command |

The whole widget catalogue opens from the "Catalogue Widgets" button in
@catalogue_app's menu; new widgets appear there first.

## Steps

1. Pick the widget that matches the request.
2. Give the user the link. For sharing in a group or with a friend, suggest
   the inline form: type `@catalogue_app card` in that chat and tap the result.
3. Under the card there are buttons to open the widget catalogue and to share
   the card further.
